# Experiment-first joint neuronal constraints

This stage prioritizes measured neuronal responses and intervention phenotypes over old muscle trajectories and model-only rhythm scores. No literature entry is automatically a passed simulation test. The inventory queue contains 126 original structure/evidence entries, including overlapping descriptions; chat-only summaries and all other brain.json namespaces remain intact. It is not a set of 126 independent experiments.

Two new path-focused candidate assays are implemented: T1 BRK→BON1 functional excitation (Sapkal 2024, DOI 10.1038/s41586-024-07854-7), and MDN→LBL40 evoked response (Feng 2020, DOI 10.1038/s41467-020-19936-x). Source identities, current annotations, original author-signed edges, input hashes and extraction protocol are saved in results/experimental-panel/protocol.json. Graphs retain all direct and two-hop source-to-target paths and all induced edges among those cells: 36 and 42 cells respectively. These are not full surrounding recurrent networks.

The BRK/BON1 published IDs are retained despite renamed types. T1 BRK IDs 10788/10564 are now AN19A018, and BON1 IDs 10441/10348 are IN27X001 in the local source; old names were AN19A009/IN27X003. Posterior BRK pairs have additional neuromere annotation conflicts and are not silently included. MDN and LBL40 use current type labels, without claiming exact experimental driver coverage. These identity/version and sex limitations prevent an unconditional pass claim.

The HH model remains two-compartment and conductance-based. No periodic muscle target is fitted. Bilateral targets of the same annotated type share parameters. The predeclared target excitatory-gain multipliers are 1, 1.5 and 2; 1 means no additional fit. Activation lasts 2 seconds, with 400 ms baseline. Assay current 160 pA is an engineering stimulus prior, not calibrated optogenetic light dose. Primary qualitative proxies are target somatic depolarization or increased firing, together with increased final model intracellular Ca. This Ca is not a calibrated GCaMP measurement; no absolute experimental spike rates are invented.

Both assay response directions were supported at all three tested multipliers. The unchanged baseline gain already gives BRK→BON1 target depolarizations about 13.6/15.3 mV, and MDN→LBL40 about 8.4/7.5 mV. Larger gain is therefore not selected simply because it increases the response. These are partial qualitative neuronal matches, not proof of stopping, backward walking, precise calcium kinetics, or the completeness of the pathways.

Commands: `python prepare_experimental_panel.py`; `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 NUMBA_NUM_THREADS=1 python experimental_panel.py`; `python experimental_priorities.py`. Use requirements.txt and the established neural environment. Simulations are deterministic. Full raw spike counts, voltage records, final Ca, configuration hashes, all failed/numerical results and parameter candidates are retained. Existing phenomena-audit.json is deliberately not overwritten: its scoped older results are not inherited by the new panel.

Next checks prioritize finer integration and alternate stimulus strengths, then verified identities for additional circuits (MF14, LUL130, steering/stop pathways), sensory presynaptic mechanisms, and behavioral closed-loop contexts. A missing mechanism is not repairable by arbitrary parameter changes; implementing it requires evidence and separate validation. No claim that most brain.json phenomena are satisfied is made.
