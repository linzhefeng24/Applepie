# Mechanisms, evidence and limits

The current implementation is new, not recovered Monday code. It is an electrically reduced two-compartment conductance model rather than EM-reconstructed morphology. Classical HH Na/K gating is an explicit kinetic prior (Hodgkin & Huxley1952, doi:10.1113/jphysiol.1952.sp004764), not a measured adult fruit-fly channel distribution or a faithful rerun of the complete Günay ModelDB model.

Somatic compartment: capacitance, leak, persistent Na, conductance synapses and injected current. Axonal compartment: capacitance, leak, transient Na activation/inactivation, delayed K activation, voltage-dependent Ca entry, intracellular calcium and calcium-activated K. The two are coupled electrically. Synapses have finite biexponential rise/decay, reversal potential and discretized axonal delay. No periodic external input, spike reset, imposed phase clock, joint servo or fabricated edge generates the neural oscillation.

For each compartment: C dV/dt = sum[g(E-V)] + coupling + injected current. Transient Na and delayed K use explicit m/h/n gates with exponential gate updates; voltage integration is implicit with frozen gates. Intracellular calcium obeys entry/removal dynamics; SK conductance depends on calcium. The model does not include all receptor kinetics, short-term plasticity, gap junctions, dynamic sodium/pump, measured branch geometry or spatially located individual synapses. Extra channels do not make the unmeasured parameters true.

Adult MN reference targets are from Azevedo et al.2020, eLife.56754: fast/intermediate/slow Rin150/300/700MOhm and reported RMP-68/-60/-48mV. Slow cells can fire about30Hz at rest; intermediate/fast are silent. No extra liquid-junction correction is applied. These are flexor class exemplars, not a demonstrated assignment of each MANC cell to a fast/intermediate/slow unit, and not automatic SETi/FETi extensor identities.

The first unconstrained passive fit used an overly depolarized intermediate leak reversal. It is retained as a failed/underconstrained candidate. Refinement constrained leak reversal and tested a candidate persistent sodium mechanism; the accepted reference reproduces the measured somatic response. Adult class fits are non-unique. Unknown EM MN subtype uses the calibrated intermediate reference, explicitly labelled; it is not described as a fully physiological per-cell reconstruction.

Spatial and synaptic quantities not measured in the specified adult cells remain priors. Structural weights are author-signed connectivity exports. Negative values do not represent negative synapse counts, and a central glutamate sign must not be copied to the neuromuscular junction.

Important numerical limitation: coarse0.025ms vs0.0125ms changed the minimum-core rhythm frequency; the failed test is preserved. Finer0.003125ms/0.0015625ms core checks and0.00625ms/0.003125ms full-T1 baseline checks are separately recorded. The full14-condition regulatory sweep is exploratory at0.025ms, not a convergence certificate for every condition. Event delays are quantized at the integration step; the requested1.5ms delay has a one-step indexing uncertainty.

Neural-to-muscle readout is a causal pooled spike filter plus nonnegative constant gains and the independently checked native MuJoCo activation law. It is NOT a calibrated receptor/vesicle/Ca-release NMJ model.104 channels have candidate anatomical pools,26 have no candidate pool; ambiguous matches remain ambiguous. Saved model-derived muscle trajectories constrain population output, not uniquely identified single-cell spike trains or absolute physiological Hz.
